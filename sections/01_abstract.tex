\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

\noindent I worked for 38 months at SAS Everbloo on \textbf{Metis}, a B2B flight booking platform. Most of my code is in \texttt{api-aerial} and \texttt{front-reservation}. The recurring difficulty was mixing Sabre/Amadeus session-based flows with short-lived NDC offers in one checkout.

\noindent I spent time on offer mapping, OAuth2 refresh, NDC order creation (passenger names, lap infants), price changes before ticketing, and multi-currency totals. Chapters below describe the code, the measurements we kept, and a few production incidents.

\vspace{0.6cm}
\noindent \textbf{\large Keywords:}\\
\noindent GDS, NDC, TypeScript, Node.js, OAuth2, ISO 4217, ADM.
